What this is Play Download

Measure what your system actually does.

This page serves the test signal. You play it through the system you want to tune, and the app listens at the seat where you actually sit. It compares what it hears to a target and tells you what to set each band on your head unit to. That is the whole product.

Play it here.

Straight from the browser, through an AUX cable or Bluetooth from a laptop. Shows you which position is running so you know when to move the phone.

Or take it with you.

WAV files for a USB stick or an SD card. This is the path most head units like best.

Three ways to use it.

Same engine underneath, three amounts of effort. Pick the one that matches how much time you have and how much you care.

  1. Speaker check. Seconds. Hold the phone about 30 cm from one speaker and read it live. Tells you what that speaker is doing, and lets you compare two of them, which is how you find the weak one.
  2. Just fix it. Two minutes. One reading at 30 cm, aimed at a sensible target, and you get a number for every band. The fewest decisions that still produce a real answer.
  3. Full measurement. Ten minutes. Five positions around the seat, averaged, then a second measurement afterwards to prove what actually changed. The only one that measures what you hear where you sit.

If your controls have no numbers on them.

Plenty of factory systems show sliders with no values. A Volvo Sensus does. Telling you to set 300 Hz to plus two is useless when there is no plus two anywhere on the screen.

So the app has a live tuning mode for those. It listens while you move a control, names which one to move and which way, and shows the distance to the target falling as you go. No numbers required at either end.

What this page will not do.

It will not measure anything. A browser cannot turn off the automatic gain control on a phone microphone, and that processing flattens level differences over time, which is exactly the thing being measured. A web page would produce a smooth, believable, mostly fictional curve with no error to catch it. So this page plays the signal and stops there.

Get the app.

Native iOS, because the measurement needs an audio session a browser cannot ask for. The app carries its own copy of this signal, so it works with no connection to this site.

It is in closed testing right now and is not on the App Store yet. If you are one of the testers, you already have it. Everything on this page works without it, and the signal is the same signal.
